# Pedja's white paper questions

Email from Pedja, reproduced from the message shared by Xinya.

---

Hello Xinya,

Here are the questions that I mentioned during our meeting:

1. What is the problem that you are trying to solve?
2. Why is it important?
3. How is it solved today, and what are the limitations of current solutions?
4. How will you solve the problem, what is new (and better) in your approach and why do you think you will be successful?
5. What will be the limitations of your solutions?
6. If successful, what would be the impact of your solution?

The length of the white paper should be <= 4 pages (without the references).

Best,

Pedja
